Add / Remove Media

Manage what shows under News → Media on the VMS site.

How saving works: this page edits a copy of data/media.json. When you're done, click Download media.json and replace the file in your site's data folder (then commit/upload as usual). Images and videos you add here are saved with the download too — put them in the media folder next to data. If the Discord sync also writes media.json, it may overwrite manual changes on its next run.
